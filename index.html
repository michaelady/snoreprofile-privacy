Privacy policy

Snore Profile for Android

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Android app Snore Profile handles information. It applies to the app distributed under the package name com.snoreprofile.app.

Developer: ADIV
Contact: michaelady10@gmail.com

Information the app collects and processes

Snore Profile processes the following information on your phone:

  • Snore audio recordings captured through the microphone while you are using a sleep or test session.
  • Sleep and snore statistics derived from those recordings, such as scores, timelines, notes, and similar session details you enter or the app calculates.
  • Device or crash data the app itself records, if any.

Where that information stays

All of this data is stored and processed only on your phone. It never leaves the device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

The app does not send recordings, statistics, or other app data over the internet. It does not request the Android internet permission, and it has no account, cloud sync, or remote processing.

Sharing and sale

No data is shared with or sold to any third party. That includes analytics, advertising, and crash-reporting services. Snore Profile does not include those services.

How long data is kept

Data is kept only on the device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Snore Profile removes all app data from the phone.

Security

Data stays in the app’s private local storage on the device, protected by Android’s app sandbox. Other apps cannot read that storage in normal use.

How to delete your data

You can remove Snore Profile data in any of these ways:

  • Delete recordings and other data inside the app.
  • Clear the app’s data in Android Settings (Apps, then Snore Profile, then Storage, then Clear data).
  • Uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s stored data from the device.

Permissions

The microphone permission is used only to detect and record snoring (and the related sleep sounds the app measures) during a session you start. The app does not use the microphone for advertising, identification, or any purpose other than that on-device detection and recording.

The app may also ask to show notifications, run a foreground microphone service while a night is in progress, keep the processor awake during monitoring, and vibrate. Those permissions support overnight recording on the phone. They are not used to send data off the device.

Children’s privacy

Snore Profile is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, contact us and delete the app data from that device using the steps above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. The updated policy applies from that date. Please review this page occasionally.
